Sendae Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 September 2026

Sendae helps you draft, schedule and publish social posts from your desktop. This policy explains how we handle personal information when you use the Sendae app, its online service and connected integrations.

Who we are

Sendae is operated by SPACEMANCODES LTD, registered in England and Wales under company number 12193639. We are responsible for the personal information described in this policy.

For privacy questions or requests, email mason@spacemancodes.com.

Information we handle

  • Your account: name, email address, a hashed password and authentication records.
  • Your workspace: workspace settings, drafts, post text, uploaded images and videos, scheduling preferences, publication history and delivery errors. Some information is stored on your device and synchronised with our servers so scheduled posts can publish while the app is closed.
  • Connected social accounts: account and Page identifiers, names, profile pictures, permissions, access tokens and, where provided, refresh tokens. We receive these from the social platforms you authorise. Where supported and authorised, we also retrieve engagement statistics for your posts.
  • Support and technical information: messages you send us and technical records needed to operate the service, such as IP addresses, request details and error logs.

Connecting a social account is optional. The permissions available depend on the platform and the features you choose. We use the platform's authorisation process; Sendae does not ask for your social-network password. Stored social access credentials are encrypted on our server.

How we use information

We use account and workspace information to provide the service you request: signing you in, synchronising your work, connecting accounts, publishing scheduled content and showing publication results. Our lawful basis is performing our contract with you.

We use technical and support information for our legitimate interests in keeping Sendae secure, reliable and working correctly, preventing abuse and answering enquiries. We may also retain or disclose information where necessary to meet legal obligations. If we rely on consent for a particular use, you can withdraw it by contacting us without affecting earlier lawful processing.

Account details are necessary to provide an online account. Without the relevant social permissions, Sendae cannot connect to or publish through that platform.

Who receives information

We send your chosen content and media to the social platforms you connect, including LinkedIn, Facebook, Threads or X, as needed to carry out your publishing instructions. Published content is visible according to the platform's settings and policies. Platforms may fetch media through temporary links.

Providers of hosting, network security, email delivery and other infrastructure process information needed to run Sendae. Cloudflare handles network traffic for sendae.app. These providers and connected platforms may process information outside the UK; locations depend on the services involved. Contact us for information about the locations and transfer safeguards applicable to your data.

If you authorise an external assistant or other client to use Sendae, it can access workspace information and perform the actions you permit. Its handling of information is covered by its own privacy policy. We may disclose information when legally required or necessary to protect the service and people's rights.

Storage, retention and deletion

We retain account and workspace information while it is needed to provide Sendae. Retention also depends on outstanding scheduled posts, synchronisation, support, security, backups and legal obligations.

Deleting a draft from the app does not immediately erase every copy: synchronisation records, publication snapshots, uploaded media and backups can remain. To request deletion of your account or retained information, email us using the address above. We will explain any information that must be retained and why.

Disconnecting a social account removes its stored access credentials from Sendae and stops pending publishing for that connection. You can also revoke access in the social platform's settings. Disconnecting or deleting information in Sendae does not remove posts already published on a social platform; manage those on the platform itself. Local copies on your device must also be managed separately.

Cookies and local storage

Sendae uses essential session and security cookies for its web flows and stores authentication and workspace information locally in the desktop app. These support sign-in, security and offline work.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, you can request access, correction, deletion, restriction or a portable copy of your personal information.

You can object to processing based on our legitimate interests. Contact mason@spacemancodes.com to exercise your rights. We may need to confirm your identity before responding.

You can also complain to the UK's Information Commissioner's Office or your local data-protection authority.

Changes

We will publish updates on this page and change the date above. Where a change materially affects how we use your information, we will take appropriate steps to bring it to your attention.
